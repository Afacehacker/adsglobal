# ADSGLOBAL Logistics & International Ads Platform

ADSGLOBAL is a dual-purpose logistics marketplace and international classified ads advertising platform. It enables users to purchase curated goods for international forwarding to family/friends abroad and post high-converting ad campaigns across global cities powered by prepaid COINS.

## 🚀 Features

- **Global Logistics Marketplace**: 9 categories including Fast Food & Express Meals, Family Gift Boxes, Electronics & Tech, Jewelry & Fine Accessories, Perfumes, Beauty & Fashion, Food & Groceries, Home & Living, Automotive & Vehicles.
- **Logistics Pricing Engine**: Real-time weight-bracket, country-specific customs clearance, handling, and express shipping calculations.
- **International Classified Ads**: Post campaigns on WhatsApp, TikTok, Facebook, Zangi, Snapchat across specific countries & cities.
- **Internal Prepaid COIN Wallet**: 1 COIN = ₦1 with manual bank deposit verification & admin approval workflow.
- **Super Admin Dashboard**: Full control over deposit approvals, order status updates, ad pricing rules, and delivery rates.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React (Vite), Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Axios, Zustand
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB (Mongoose), JWT Authentication
